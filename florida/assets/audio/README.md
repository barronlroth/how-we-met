# Florida soundtrack

`im-so-high-chiptune.mp3` is the completed chiptune arrangement of Grind Mode's
“I'm So High,” supplied for this game by the user. It is copied without changing
the audio from `Grind Mode - Im So High - Chiptune.mp3` produced by the separate
chiptune task on September 10, 2026.

- Duration: 348.943673 seconds (5:49).
- Format: stereo MP3, 44.1 kHz, 13,959,947 bytes.
- SHA-256: `b09ab8e2fbae0a31560a612d81c0aa8a896a799362a748186a628c05e53ec7ff`.

Sound defaults on; the browser starts streaming at the first click, tap or
keypress. Choosing mute first keeps it silent. It loops through the menu,
race and finish screen, retains its position across runs, and pauses with the
game or a hidden tab. The soundtrack has its own gain under the existing master
sound control; engine, wind and event effects remain synthesized separately.
